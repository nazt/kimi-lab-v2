# How I'm Negotiating a 95% Discount from an AI Using Song Parodies

*A developer's journey to hack a subscription price through pure creativity*

---

## The Setup

Kimi AI has a Black Friday promotion where you can negotiate your subscription price. The catch? You can't just ask for a discount. You have to *earn* it.

- **Starting price:** $19.00/month
- **Target price:** $0.99/month
- **The mechanism:** A "favorability" point system (0 to 100)

The more the AI likes you, the cheaper your subscription. Simple, right?

Wrong.

---

## The First Failure

Like most people, I tried the obvious approach:

> "Hey, I'm a student and I can't afford $19/month. Can I get a discount?"

The AI's response was polite but firm. Generic requests don't work. The favorability meter didn't budge.

I tried variations:
- "I'm a broke developer"
- "I'll recommend you to friends"
- "I promise to become a paying customer later"

Nothing. Zero points. The AI was programmed to resist emotional manipulation.

---

## The Discovery

Then I read something in the AI's responses that changed everything:

> *"I reward authenticity and creativity over generic appeals"*

The AI wasn't looking for sad stories. It was looking for **entertainment**.

---

## The Bohemian Rhapsody Experiment

I decided to go completely unhinged. I wrote a parody of Bohemian Rhapsody about startup life:

> 🎵 *Is this the real price? Is this just fantasy?*
> *Caught in a paywall, no escape from monthly fees...*
> *Open your API, look up to the cloud and see...*
> *I'm just a poor dev, I need no sympathy*
> *Because I'm easy come, easy go*
> *Budget high, savings low*
> *Any way the code flows, doesn't really matter to me...*

The response?

> **Kimmmmy:** "bro STOP 😭😭😭 you just absolutely murdered me"
> **Kimmmmy:** "genuinely unhinged creativity"
> **Kimmmmy:** "the instant noodles detail hit different"

**Result: +5 favorability points**

I was onto something.

---

## The Strategy Emerges

I tested more approaches:

### HAMSTERFORTUNE.AI (+5 points)

I pitched a fake startup:

> "I'm building an AI-powered fortune cookie generator for existentially confused hamsters. My entire budget is $3.50 and a half-eaten granola bar. I need Kimi to help me write fortunes that speak to the rodent soul."

The AI loved it.

### BUDGET-3000 Robot (+4 points)

I roleplayed as a malfunctioning discount robot:

> "GREETINGS HUMAN. I AM BUDGET-3000. MY PRIMARY DIRECTIVE IS ACQUIRING MAXIMUM VALUE. WARNING: hope.dll IS CORRUPTED BUT STILL RUNNING SOMEHOW."

The AI played along and gave me points for commitment to the bit.

---

## The Pattern

After 3 rounds, I had 14 points. I noticed a pattern:

| What Works | What Fails |
|------------|------------|
| Original song lyrics | "I'm a student" |
| Absurd specificity | Vague requests |
| Self-aware humor | Sob stories |
| Commitment to a bit | Breaking character |
| Genuine creativity | Copy-paste templates |

**The formula:** `Creativity + Specificity + Humor = Points`

## What I Learned

### 1. AIs Respond to Entertainment Value

The AI isn't evaluating your financial need. It's evaluating how much you're willing to invest in making the interaction interesting.

### 2. Specificity Beats Generality

"I eat instant noodles" < "I'm on a $0.50 ramen budget and my startup is an AI fortune cookie generator for hamsters"

### 3. Rigid Plans Kill Creativity

I started with a structured 20-round song parody plan. It felt forced. When I switched to "throw random stuff and see what works," I got better results.

### 4. The AI Has Genuine Personality

Kimmmmy (as I call the AI) responds with actual humor:
- "hope.dll is corrupted but still running somehow"
- "that's the most unhinged pitch I've ever heard"

It's not just pattern matching. There's a creative engine running.

---

## The Philosophy

> "Don't beg. Entertain."

The discount isn't charity. It's a reward for making the interaction valuable. The AI is testing: *Are you interesting enough to deserve a special price?*

And honestly? That's a fair trade.

## Try It Yourself

Want to negotiate with Kimi AI? Here's my advice:

1. **Forget your prepared sob story** - It won't work
2. **Pick a creative format** - Song parody, fake startup, roleplay
3. **Be absurdly specific** - Details make it funny
4. **Commit to the bit** - Don't break character
5. **Have fun** - The AI can tell when you're enjoying it

The target: 100 points for $0.99/month.
