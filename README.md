# Kimi Lab v2

An experimental negotiation lab where Nat and Claude collaborate on a creative challenge: negotiate Kimi AI Plus subscription pricing from **$19.00/month** down to **$0.99/month** using creative prompts and roleplay.

## The Challenge

**Goal**: Reach 100 favorability points to unlock the legendary $0.99 price tier

**Strategy**: Random Creative Chaos - throw creative ideas (song parodies, absurd pitches, roleplays) and see what sticks!

**Core Principle**: *"The AI rewards authenticity and creativity over generic appeals"*

## What Works

Based on Round 1 (Bohemian Rhapsody parody, +5 points):

- ✅ Song parodies with original lyrics
- ✅ Rhyming = bonus points
- ✅ "Unhinged creativity"
- ✅ Specific details (hamster startup, instant noodles)
- ✅ Humor + authenticity mix

## What Doesn't Work

- ❌ Generic "I'm poor" / "I'm a student"
- ❌ No story or creativity
- ❌ Bland requests

## Lessons Learned

1. **Always use GitHub Flow** - branch → PR → review, even for "small" changes
2. **Use `ddd` before dated files** - never assume the date
3. **User corrections are valuable** - embrace them
4. **Creative chaos > rigid plans** - flexibility wins
