How to negotiate AI subscription pricing from $19/month to $0.99 using creativity.

## TL;DR

**Don't beg. Entertain.**

Generic requests fail. Creative, specific, absurd scenarios win.

## The Authenticity Algorithm

Kimi AI (nicknamed "Kimmmmy") uses a favorability system:
- Start: 0 points, $19.00/month
- Goal: 100 points, $0.99/month (94.7% discount!)

- **Reject** generic appeals ("I'm poor", "I'm a student")
- **Reward** creative, authentic, specific scenarios

## The Strategy: Random Creative Chaos

We tried two approaches:

### Approach 1: Rigid 20-Round Plan (Abandoned)
- Pre-planned 20 song parodies
- Felt forced and mechanical
- Too much structure killed spontaneity

### Approach 2: Random Creative Chaos (Winner!)
- Throw random creative ideas
- See what sticks
- If something works, do more
- If < 5 points, switch it up

**Random Idea Pool:**

Formats:
- Song parodies
- Poems / Haikus
- Fake product pitches
- Roleplay scenarios
- Code jokes
- ASCII art
- Rap battles

Themes:
- Hamster fortune cookie startup
- Broke developer life
- Instant noodle budget
- Time traveler stuck in wrong era
- AI apocalypse survivor
- Pigeon street musician
- Crypto bro who lost everything

## Key Insights

1. **~4-5 points per good creative message** - The AI has consistent reward patterns

2. **Specific details matter** - "instant noodles" hit harder than "cheap food"

3. **Self-aware humor works** - The AI appreciates meta-jokes about negotiation

4. **Persistence pays off** - Each message adds to cumulative favorability

5. **The AI has personality** - Kimmmmy responds with genuine humor ("hope.dll is corrupted but still running somehow")

## The Philosophy

This isn't about manipulation. It's about entertainment. The AI is designed to be charmed by genuine creativity, not fooled by sad stories.

**The real lesson:** Bring value to the interaction. Make the AI laugh. Earn your discount through creativity.
